## What is Defeat Anime RNG?

Defeat Anime RNG is a Roblox experience by **Defeat Anime Bosses**. The developer's listing describes a collection-and-combat loop: roll for units, use those units against anime bosses, merge units to raise their levels, survive waves and unlock additional areas. Rare units and mutations are also mentioned.

This page is based on a supplied capture of the developer listing. The capture was reviewed on September 17, 2026. The editor has **not completed an in-game test**, so this page does not assert exact button positions, roll odds, unlock requirements or a best-unit ranking.
